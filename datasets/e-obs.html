E-OBS

Gridded station analysisKNMI / ECA&D for the Copernicus Climate Change Service

E-OBS daily gridded observational dataset for Europe. Gridded from ECA&D station series. Full versions come out twice a year with provisional monthly updates. Station coverage can change between versions, which can leave gaps or steps in recent years.

Current versionv34.0e (released 29 October 2026; covers 1950-01-01 to 2026-06-30)
CoverageEurope; 1950 to present; daily; 0.1° and 0.25°; 20-member ensemble
VariablesDaily temperature (mean, min, max), precipitation, pressure, humidity, wind, radiation

E-OBS v34.0e precipitation, northern Morocco from February 2006

AcknowledgeddatasetSignificance: MediumReported 2026-11-09

Every Moroccan precipitation station in E-OBS within 34–39°N, 10–3.5°W stops on 7 February 2006 (Tangier airport, Al Hoceima, Taza and Rabat-Salé); afterwards the only station on the African side is Ceuta, and the Moroccan grid cells are filled from distant stations. From then on those cells change character while the Iberian cells do not: their October–March maximum daily rainfall (box mean) averages 33 mm in the 2000s, 48 mm in the 2010s and 55 mm in the 2020s (Iberian cells 23, 27, 29 mm), and their E-OBS ensemble spread on wet days rises from 5.3–6.1 mm before 2005 to 6.9–8.0 mm, while Iberia's falls. The Moroccan cells carry about half of the rainfall-extremes trend over the box used by World Weather Attribution Report No. 83. Whether rainfall over northern Morocco really changed cannot be told from E-OBS after 2006.

E-OBS v34.0e precipitation, Greece 2005–2014

AcknowledgeddatasetSignificance: MediumReported 2026-11-21

Public ECA&D Greek stations with near-complete years fall from about 10 to 1–2 in 2005–2013 and return to 5–7 from 2014. E-OBS Greek precipitation drops by about 35–45% over exactly those years against both CPC Unified (ratio 0.71–0.83 → 0.40–0.60 → 0.60–0.84) and GPCP (0.55–0.66 → 0.32–0.48 → 0.56–0.79). In 2006 E-OBS has 239 mm over the box, against 599 mm (CPC) and 736 mm (GPCP).

GPCP Monthly

Satellite–gauge mergeGPCP (University of Maryland / NASA GSFC) for the NOAA Climate Data Record program

Global Precipitation Climatology Project (GPCP) Monthly Climate Data Record, Version 2.3. Merges passive-microwave and infrared satellite estimates with GPCC gauge analyses over land.

Current versionVersion 2.3
CoverageGlobal land and ocean; January 1979 to present; monthly; 2.5°
VariablesMonthly precipitation and error estimates

GHCN-CAMS

Gridded station analysisNOAA Climate Prediction Center (CPC); distributed by NOAA PSL

CPC GHCN-CAMS Gridded 2 m Temperature (Land). Combines GHCN and CAMS station data with lapse-rate topographic adjustment, so it can be updated in near real time.

Current versionOperational (updated monthly)
CoverageGlobal land; 1948 to present; monthly; 0.5°
VariablesMonthly land surface air temperature

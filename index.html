The register

Each entry describes a problem found in a published climate paper, dataset or piece of software, with the evidence to reproduce it, the fix we proposed where we could (how we propose fixes), and the response of the authors or developers. Nothing appears here until they have replied or three weeks have passed.

The analyses behind these entries were carried out with an AI system (Claude, Anthropic) under the author's direction. The author checked the methods and results and is responsible for the content.

Using a dataset or paper and have a question? Open its discussion page by DOI, search the register and discussions, or write to us. Researchers can ask each other, the authors or the maintainer.

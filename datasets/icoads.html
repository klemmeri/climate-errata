ICOADS

Marine observation archiveNOAA NCEI, NCAR, NOC Southampton and partners

International Comprehensive Ocean-Atmosphere Data Set, Release 3. The archive of marine surface reports behind HadSST, ERSST and other SST and marine products.

Current versionR3.0.0 (1662–2014), R3.1.0 (with subsidiary records), near-real-time R3.0.2 (2015 onwards)
CoverageGlobal oceans; 1662 to present; individual reports
VariablesIndividual ship, buoy and platform reports: SST, air temperature, wind, pressure, humidity, cloud

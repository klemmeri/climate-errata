ERA5

ReanalysisECMWF for the Copernicus Climate Change Service (C3S)

ECMWF Reanalysis v5 (ERA5). The most widely used global reanalysis. Near-real-time data (ERA5T) can be revised if a production problem is found.

Current versionERA5 (with ERA5T near-real-time extension)
CoverageGlobal; January 1940 to near present; hourly; 0.25° on the Climate Data Store
VariablesAtmosphere, land and ocean-wave variables

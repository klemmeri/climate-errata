Clean checks: Mauritsen 2025 (energy imbalance doubled), Nerem 2018 (sea-level acceleration), Dangendorf 2026 (coastal extremes), Xu 2026 CEE (drought), Guerreiro 2018 (hourly extremes), IGCC 2025 marine-heatwave indicator

No error foundpaperSignificance: Not scored

What it is

Six widely cited studies we tested independently and found to hold: Earth's energy imbalance doubling (Mauritsen 2025), sea-level acceleration (Nerem 2018), coastal extremes (Dangendorf 2026), drought (Xu 2026), hourly rainfall extremes (Guerreiro 2018) and the IGCC 2025 marine-heatwave indicator.

Finding

Claims hold under independent tests (6 studies)

Authors and producers: respond or object

If you are an author, data producer or developer of this work and you disagree with this finding, or think we have misread something, send us your response. We review every response promptly. If it shows our finding is wrong, we correct or withdraw the entry openly (see Corrections). If you would like your response shown on this page, it is published unedited, with or without your name as you prefer, and never without your permission. You can also reply publicly in the discussion below.

Discussion

Questions and discussion are welcome: ask other readers, the authors or the maintainer. Authors and developers are especially welcome to reply. Comments use GitHub Discussions (sign in with GitHub). No GitHub account? Write to us.

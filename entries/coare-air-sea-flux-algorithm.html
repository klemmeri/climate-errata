COARE air–sea flux algorithm (CMEPS, E3SM, NorESM)

ConfirmedcodeSignificance: Medium

What it is

The COARE algorithm calculates the exchange of heat, moisture and momentum between the ocean surface and the atmosphere (air–sea fluxes). Versions of it are used in the coupling code of climate models such as CESM (CMEPS), E3SM and NorESM.

Finding

Sign error in the COARE flux code

VerdictReal error
SignificanceMedium (Size 1 · Reach 2 · Consequence 1 = 4 of 6)
Basis: Sign error size not yet quantified (S=1 provisional); CMEPS/E3SM/NorESM coupled models (R); fix scheduled, no rerun (C)
OutcomeConfirmed
Where reportedIssues CMEPS #717, E3SM #9075, NorESM #859
DateBefore Nov 4

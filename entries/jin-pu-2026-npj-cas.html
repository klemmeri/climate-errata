Jin & Pu 2026, npj CAS (warming acceleration)

ResolvedpaperSignificance: Medium

What it is

Jin and Pu (2026, npj Climate and Atmospheric Science) tested whether the rate of global warming has accelerated, using change-point searches on global temperature records.

Finding

CIs too narrow; split search inflates false positives

VerdictReal (partly); author showed split was a priori
SignificanceMedium (Size 1 · Reach 1 · Consequence 1 = 3 of 6)
Basis: CIs too narrow (S); single npj paper (R); author showed split a priori (C); reconciled: R1 recent npj paper, C1 author conceded points
OutcomeResolved
Where reportedZenodo preprint + email
Our write-up on Zenodo (latest version): doi:10.5281/zenodo.24046533

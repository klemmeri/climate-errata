COOP Hourly Precipitation (HPD)

Station networkNOAA National Centers for Environmental Information (NCEI)

NOAA Cooperative Observer Program (COOP) Precipitation Data – Hourly, Version 2 (C-HPD). Fischer & Porter gauges of the COOP network. Between 2004 and 2013 the stations were upgraded from paper punch tape to electronic data loggers.

Current versionVersion 2
CoverageUnited States and territories; about 2,000 stations; 1940 to present
VariablesHourly and 15-minute precipitation

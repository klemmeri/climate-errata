CPC Unified Gauge Analysis

Gridded gauge analysisNOAA Climate Prediction Center (CPC); distributed by NOAA PSL

CPC Unified Gauge-Based Analysis of Global Daily Precipitation. Daily gauge analysis built by optimal interpolation. The retrospective period uses more than 30,000 gauges and the real-time version about 17,000. Quality varies with gauge density, and the files carry the gauge count for each cell. MERRA-2 uses it to correct the precipitation that reaches its land surface.

Current versionRetrospective version 1979–2005; real-time version 2006–present
CoverageGlobal land; 1979 to present; daily; 0.5°
VariablesDaily precipitation and number of gauges per cell

NASA MERRA-2 corrected precipitation (PRECTOTCORR) over Australia

ConfirmeddatasetSignificance: HighReported 2026-11-20

About a 20% dry step from 2016 in PRECTOTCORR over Australia, inherited from the CPC Unified gauge analysis (CPCU) that MERRA-2 uses to correct land precipitation. CPCU's own gauge-count field shows Australia lost about 95% of its gauges in two steps (2007–08 and 2015–16, e.g. E Australia ~590 → 90). GPCP and GPCC do not show the step, and a gauge-independent reanalysis (NCEP R1) confirms it sits in CPCU.

IBTrACS

Tropical cyclone best tracksNOAA National Centers for Environmental Information (NCEI)

International Best Track Archive for Climate Stewardship, Version 4 (v04r01). Combines best tracks from the warning agencies worldwide. Recent storms are provisional and can change.

Current versionv04r01
CoverageGlobal; 1840s to present; generally 3-hourly
VariablesStorm position, intensity and size from all warning agencies
Known issuesCaveats are in the technical documentation; bug fixes are recorded in the v04r01 change log on the NCEI page.

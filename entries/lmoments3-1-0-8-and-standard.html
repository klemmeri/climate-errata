lmoments3 1.0.8 (Ouranos; L-moment engine behind xclim PWM fits) and standard_precip SPI

Reported (open)codeSignificance: Medium

What it is

lmoments3 is the Python library (maintained by Ouranos) that fits extreme-value distributions by L-moments; it is the engine behind xclim's distribution fits. standard_precip is a Python package for computing the Standardized Precipitation Index.

Finding

Wakeby distribution: quantile/CDF/PDF use the scale-dependent γ unscaled and reject valid δ<0, so 77–85% of fitted 100-yr values are NaN or off by ~50×; standard_precip SPI with dist_type='wak' inherits it (half of months NaN, 78% 'severe drought' vs 5%)

VerdictReal error
SignificanceMedium (Size 2 · Reach 1 · Consequence 1 = 4 of 6)
Basis: Quantiles NaN or 10–60× wrong vs Hosking's R lmom (S); Wakeby niche but reachable via xclim and standard_precip (R); errors gross enough to notice, risk in automated pipelines (C)

The register

Each entry describes a problem found in a published climate paper, dataset or piece of software, with the evidence to reproduce it and the response of the authors or developers. Nothing appears here until they have replied or three weeks have passed.

Data: entries.json (free to reuse under CC BY 4.0).

Maintained by Mark L. Morrissey, Ph.D., Professor Emeritus of Meteorology. Content CC BY 4.0; code MIT. Corrections to our own entries are made openly and dated.

OISST v2.1

SST analysisNOAA National Centers for Environmental Information (NCEI)

NOAA 0.25° Daily Optimum Interpolation Sea Surface Temperature (OISST), Version 2.1. Blends satellite (AVHRR) and in situ SST. Widely used for marine heatwave studies.

Current versionv2.1 (released March 2020)
CoverageGlobal oceans; September 1981 to present; daily; 0.25°
VariablesDaily SST, anomalies, error estimate, sea-ice concentration

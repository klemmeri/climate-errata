GPCC Monthly

Gridded gauge analysisGlobal Precipitation Climatology Centre (GPCC), Deutscher Wetterdienst

GPCC Precipitation Analysis Monthly, Version 2025. The main global monthly gauge analysis. It is also the gauge input to GPCP and to the IMERG Final Run.

Current versionVersion 2025 (successor of Full Data Monthly V2022; now merged with the former Monitoring Product)
CoverageGlobal land; 1891 to present; monthly; 0.25°, 0.5°, 1.0°, 2.5°
VariablesMonthly land precipitation and number of gauges per cell

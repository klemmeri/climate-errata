NASA MERRA-2 corrected precipitation (PRECTOTCORR) over Australia

ConfirmeddatasetSignificance: High

Finding

About a 20% dry step from 2016 in PRECTOTCORR over Australia, inherited from the CPC Unified gauge analysis (CPCU) that MERRA-2 uses to correct land precipitation. CPCU's own gauge-count field shows Australia lost about 95% of its gauges in two steps (2007–08 and 2015–16, e.g. E Australia ~590 → 90). GPCP and GPCC do not show the step, and a gauge-independent reanalysis (NCEP R1) confirms it sits in CPCU.

VerdictReal error (input-data step)
SignificanceHigh (Size 2 · Reach 2 · Consequence 1 = 5 of 6)
Basis: ~20% step in a continental precipitation series, larger than trend signals (S); MERRA-2 is a major reanalysis, PRECTOTCORR forces land and soil-moisture products (R); developers confirmed, no rerun of published work (C)
OutcomeConfirmed
Where reportedEmail to NASA GMAO (M. Bosilovich, R. Reichle); confirmed by Reichle. MERRA-2 is frozen and will not be changed; MERRA-21C and SMAP L4_SM v6+ no longer use CPCU; to be noted in the MERRA-21C validation documentation

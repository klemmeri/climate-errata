MERRA-2

ReanalysisNASA Global Modeling and Assimilation Office (GMAO); distributed by GES DISC

Modern-Era Retrospective analysis for Research and Applications, Version 2. NASA's global reanalysis. Land precipitation seen by the land model (PRECTOTCORR) is corrected towards gauge-based analyses, so changes in those analyses can pass into MERRA-2.

Current versionMERRA-2 (GEOS 5.12.4)
CoverageGlobal; 1980 to near present
VariablesAtmosphere, aerosols, land surface; corrected precipitation (PRECTOTCORR) forced by gauge analyses
Known issuesWe did not find a public known-issues list for MERRA-2 (checked 22 Nov 2026). GMAO takes reports through its diagnostic feedback page.

NASA MERRA-2 corrected precipitation (PRECTOTCORR) over Australia

ConfirmeddatasetSignificance: HighReported 2026-11-20

About a 20% dry step from 2016 in PRECTOTCORR over Australia, inherited from the CPC Unified gauge analysis (CPCU) that MERRA-2 uses to correct land precipitation. CPCU's own gauge-count field shows Australia lost about 95% of its gauges in two steps (2007–08 and 2015–16, e.g. E Australia ~590 → 90). GPCP and GPCC do not show the step, and a gauge-independent reanalysis (NCEP R1) confirms it sits in CPCU.

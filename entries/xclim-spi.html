xclim SPI (Ouranos)

ConfirmedcodeSignificance: Medium

What it is

xclim is a Python library from Ouranos (Montreal) for computing climate indices from model and observational data. Its SPI function converts rainfall totals into a standardized drought index.

Finding

Default 3-parameter gamma fit collapses its location onto the sample minimum (SPI −8.21)

VerdictReal error
SignificanceMedium (Size 2 · Reach 1 · Consequence 1 = 4 of 6)
Basis: SPI -8.21 outside physical range (S); xclim standard in climate services (R); no rerun (C)
OutcomeConfirmed
Where reportedGitHub #2456

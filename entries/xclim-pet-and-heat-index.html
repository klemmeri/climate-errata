xclim PET (Thornthwaite) and heat index

Fixed – not yet releasedcodeSignificance: Medium

What it is

xclim's potential evapotranspiration (PET) functions estimate how much water the atmosphere could take up; the Thornthwaite method uses temperature only. Its heat index combines temperature and humidity into an apparent temperature.

Finding

Heat index recomputed each year halves warming-driven PET increases; bare Rothfusz heat index

VerdictReal error
SignificanceMedium (Size 2 · Reach 1 · Consequence 1 = 4 of 6)
Basis: Halves warming-driven PET increase (S); xclim (R); fixed, no rerun (C)

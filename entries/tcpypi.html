tcpyPI (hurricane potential intensity)

Fixed – releasedcodeSignificance: Low

What it is

tcpyPI is the Python implementation of the potential-intensity algorithm (Bister and Emanuel), which estimates the maximum intensity a hurricane could reach given ocean and atmospheric conditions.

Finding

Published fix never implemented; zero-CAPE outflow case

VerdictReal error
SignificanceLow (Size 0 · Reach 1 · Consequence 1 = 2 of 6)
Basis: Mean Vmax change -0.17 m/s, <1% of PI (S); tcpyPI standard PI code (R); fixed, no rerun (C)
OutcomeFixed – released
Fixed on2026-11-18
Fixed inv1.5.0 (PR #91, merged and released 18 Nov 2026)
Affected versionsv1.4.2 and earlier (first affected version not checked)
Is the fix retroactive?Forward only. Results computed with affected versions are not corrected; rerun with the fixed version or later.

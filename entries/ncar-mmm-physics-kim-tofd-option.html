NCAR MMM-physics (WRF/MPAS) KIM TOFD option

ConfirmedcodeSignificance: Medium

What it is

MMM-physics is NCAR's shared library of physics schemes for the WRF and MPAS weather models. The KIM TOFD option represents turbulent orographic form drag (the drag of small-scale terrain on the wind).

Finding

Friction velocity 28–41% high; uninitialized variable

VerdictReal error
SignificanceMedium (Size 2 · Reach 0 · Consequence 1 = 3 of 6)
Basis: Friction velocity 28-41% high (S); optional non-default WRF/MPAS option (R); no published use shown (C); reconciled: C1, author confirmed bug (v2 rule)

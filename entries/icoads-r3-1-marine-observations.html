ICOADS R3.1 marine observations

AcknowledgeddatasetSignificance: Medium

What it is

ICOADS (International Comprehensive Ocean-Atmosphere Data Set) is the main archive of ship, buoy and other marine surface observations since the 1600s. It underlies sea-surface-temperature datasets such as HadSST4 and ERSST.

Finding

~295,000 position-error duplicates; some HadSST4 box-months rest on misplaced copies

VerdictReal error
SignificanceMedium (Size 1 · Reach 2 · Consequence 1 = 4 of 6)
Basis: Some HadSST4 box-months on misplaced copies (S); ICOADS is the global marine archive (R); acknowledged, not rerun (C)
OutcomeAcknowledged
Where reportedZenodo preprint
Our write-up on Zenodo (latest version): doi:10.5281/zenodo.24092539

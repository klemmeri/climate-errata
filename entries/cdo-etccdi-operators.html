CDO etccdi operators

Reported (open)codeSignificance: Medium

What it is

CDO (Climate Data Operators) is a widely used command-line toolkit for processing climate model and gridded data. Its etccdi operators compute the standard ETCCDI climate-extremes indices.

Finding

Same missing bootstrap; rx1daymon/rx5daymon return unaggregated data; other bugs

VerdictReal error
SignificanceMedium (Size 1 · Reach 2 · Consequence 1 = 4 of 6)
Basis: Missing bootstrap plus unaggregated monthly output (S); CDO is the dominant climate-data tool (R); no rerun (C)
OutcomeOpen
Where reportedCDO Redmine #12421

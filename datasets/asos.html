ASOS

Station networkNWS, FAA and Department of Defense; archived by NOAA NCEI

Automated Surface Observing System. The main US automated airport network, used in station climate records such as HadISD. Sensor replacements show up as steps in long series.

Current versionOperational network (sensor changes over time)
CoverageUnited States; about 950 mostly airport stations; 1990s to present
VariablesWind, temperature, dew point, pressure, precipitation, visibility, sky condition
Known issuesThere is no single known-issues list for ASOS climate series; sensor-change dates come from NWS and station metadata.

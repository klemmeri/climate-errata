Ask for a check of your own work

If you are an author, we will check the statistics and data handling in your published paper or public preprint, free and in confidence. Nothing is posted unless you ask us to.

Unpublished manuscripts: not accepted for now. The analysis runs on an AI service in the cloud (see Confidentiality), and until we can tell you exactly how files sent there are stored and for how long, we will not take unpublished or embargoed work. Published papers and public preprints, with their public code and data, are fine.

What we offer

  • What we look at: a careful, independent read of the parts that reviewers often have no time to check: trend and significance tests, autocorrelation and effective sample size, multiple testing, event definitions, baselines, and whether the datasets used are homogeneous over the period.
  • Reruns, not just reading. Where your code and data allow, we rerun the key results and test them, for example with null simulations, alternative datasets or a matched comparison.
  • Grid scale and observations. Comparing point, gridded and model data of different spatial resolution (change of support) is a particular strength.
  • When it helps: for example before a follow-up paper, a revised preprint or a dataset release.

Confidentiality

  • Where your files go. The analysis is done with an AI model (Anthropic's Claude) working in a cloud workspace, so papers, code and data sent for a check are uploaded to and processed on that service, not only on the editor's own computer. They are not shared with anyone else, are not posted, and are deleted from the workspace when the check is done or on request. The AI provider's own data-handling terms also apply.
  • If that is not acceptable (for example, restricted data), say so when you write. We will then either decline or limit the check to what can be done without uploading your files, such as reading the methods and discussing them with you.
  • Nothing about the check appears on this site unless you ask for it, including the fact that you asked.
  • If we find a problem, you decide what to do with it. A confidential check of your own work never leads to a register entry.
  • This is not peer review for any journal, and we are not affiliated with any publisher.

What you get

  • A short written report: what we checked, how, what held up and what did not, with the code we used.
  • If your main results reproduce, an independent reproduction statement that you may cite or link to.

Independent reproduction statements

When we reproduce a result, from your work or from our own screening of published papers, we offer the authors a short statement that can be cited or linked. It is published here only if the authors want it to be. Each statement says:

Independent reproduction — Climate Errata
Paper: authors, year, journal, DOI
What was reproduced: the specific result (figure, table or number)
How: data versions and code used; whether the authors' code or an independent implementation
Result: reproduced, with the published and our values side by side
Scope: covers only the result listed; it is not a review of the paper as a whole
Date, link to the code, and checked by: Mark L. Morrissey, Ph.D.

Limits

  • Capacity is small: one person, with an AI model doing much of the coding and analysis, and every result checked by him. We may need to decline or queue requests.
  • A check reduces the chance of an error; it cannot rule one out.
  • We decline requests where we have a conflict of interest, and say so.

How to ask

Use Write to us and choose "Please check my own work". Tell us what the paper is, what stage it is at, and which results matter most. We reply within a week to arrange how to share files.

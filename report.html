Write to us

Ask a question, send a comment without a GitHub account, or report a suspected error privately. Messages go to the maintainer only. Nothing you send is posted without your permission.

Reporting a suspected error? We check it ourselves first. If it holds up, the authors or data producers are contacted privately and given at least three weeks before anything is posted (see the posting policy). You will be credited on the entry only if you ask to be.

Your email is used only to reply to you. It is never published or shared. See the privacy policy.

E-OBS v34.0e precipitation, Greece 2005–2014

AcknowledgeddatasetSignificance: Medium

Finding

Public ECA&D Greek stations with near-complete years fall from about 10 to 1–2 in 2005–2013 and return to 5–7 from 2014. E-OBS Greek precipitation drops by about 35–45% over exactly those years against both CPC Unified (ratio 0.71–0.83 → 0.40–0.60 → 0.60–0.84) and GPCP (0.55–0.66 → 0.32–0.48 → 0.56–0.79). In 2006 E-OBS has 239 mm over the box, against 599 mm (CPC) and 736 mm (GPCP).

VerdictReal error (station gap)
SignificanceMedium (Size 2 · Reach 1 · Consequence 1 = 4 of 6)
Basis: About 35–45% regional dry bias for nine years (S2); E-OBS is a main European dataset but the issue is regional (R1); no published result rerun yet (C1)
OutcomeAcknowledged
Where reportedEmail to ECA&D/KNMI and the World Weather Attribution Report No. 83 authors, 21 Nov 2026. ECA&D/KNMI replied on 23 Nov 2026 that the issue will be added to the E-OBS Known Issues page.
Date2026-11-23

Summary

For 2005–2013, E-OBS precipitation over Greece is about 35–45% too low. The drop starts and ends when the public ECA&D Greek stations disappear from and return to the record. E-OBS's Known Issues page warns that station density is low in Greece and Turkey and changes over time. This entry gives the years of the gap and the size of its effect.

  • Example: in 2006 E-OBS gives 239 mm over the box, against 599 mm in CPC Unified and 736 mm in GPCP. The one long-running station averaged 550–630 mm in 2006–08.
  • The steps appear against both references independently, in the same years and with the same sign.

Who is affected

Studies of Greek precipitation that use E-OBS across 2005 or 2014: trends, climatologies, rainy-day counts and comparisons with reanalyses.

Response

Reported to ECA&D/KNMI on 21 November 2026 (with the authors of World Weather Attribution Report No. 83 copied). On 23 November ECA&D agreed that changes in network density strongly affect the interpolated precipitation in Greece. They explained that Greek station data are held by several organisations, which makes the problem hard to fix, and said the issue will be added to the E-OBS Known Issues page.

What users can do

For Greece in 2005–2013, check E-OBS against CPC Unified, GPCP or ERA5-Land, and avoid trends that span the gap.

Reproduce

E-OBS v34.0e rr (0.25° ensemble mean) summed to monthly totals and averaged to 1°; CPC Unified daily (NOAA PSL); GPCP v2.3; station history from the public ECA&D blend (ECA_blend_source_rr.txt). Scripts on request.
